The Story

Built from
Scratch.

Ahmad Mullick is one of Pakistan's most inspiring young entrepreneurs. He took the concept of Airbnb — largely untapped in Pakistan — and scaled it to $300,000 per year, proving that big wins are possible from Karachi to the world.

Beyond Airbnb, Ahmad is a winner of Race Wars Pakistan (@racewars.pk), a passionate builder of businesses, and a content creator who breaks down real strategies so others can follow the same path.

His content cuts through the noise — no fluff, no fake gurus. Just real numbers, real lessons, and a blueprint for the next generation of Pakistani entrepreneurs.
