No. Many of Ahmad's most successful students started with zero business experience. You just need commitment and willingness to execute. Ahmad handles the knowledge — you bring the drive.

Ahmad will show you how to start with as little as possible. One of his core strategies involves co-hosting (managing other people's properties) which requires near-zero startup capital. He'll guide you to the right entry point for your budget.

Yes. Ahmad has students in Lahore, Karachi, Islamabad, Faisalabad, and beyond. The principles apply everywhere in Pakistan. He'll help you assess your specific market during the programme.

The Airbnb Mentorship focuses specifically on short-term rental businesses. The Strategy Call and Business Builder cover any business model — Ahmad has experience in multiple verticals beyond Airbnb.

Payment is handled directly via WhatsApp after your discovery call. Ahmad accepts bank transfer and various payment methods. Reach out on WhatsApp to get started.
